B. Business overview (plain English)

Same content as BUSINESS_REQUIREMENTS.txt.

PayProSys — What the system does

  • Banks work with companies (called corporates here). Those companies upload employee payroll from spreadsheets so the bank can see what needs to be paid.
  • Portal users log in. Employees on the spreadsheet are not separate accounts—they are rows in the file.

Who does what — big picture

  • Super Admin (platform) — Sets up banks; can view users across the system when helpful.
  • Bank — Bank Admin and Bank User only see corporates that belong to their bank; they help onboard companies and bank-side users.
  • Corporate — Corporate Admin and Corporate User work for one company: upload payroll and manage batches and payments for that company only.

Signing in

  • Email and password.
  • A role (Super Admin, Bank Admin, Bank User, Corporate Admin, Corporate User) controls menus and visibility.
  • The demo intentionally keeps passwords and hosting simple—not production-grade security.

Super Admin

  • Create and manage banks.
  • Open a Users list for supporting or narrating demos.
  • Not tied to a single bank/corporate for daily payroll tasks.

Bank

  • See only corporates linked to their bank.
  • Bank Admin can create a corporate (+ corporate admin) and manage bank-side users.
  • Bank User has a similar corporates view; less “full setup” than Bank Admin.

Corporate

  • Corporate Admin may add Corporate User accounts at the same company.
  • Everyone on the corporate side sees only their own company.

Payroll upload (spreadsheet)

  • Excel .xlsx.
  • Expected columns include employee name, account number, joining date, ID, amount, and what the payment is for (salary, reimbursement, etc.). A sample sheet ships with the repo.
  • User picks which month the payroll is for.

Batches — submit and delete

  • Each upload creates a batch.
  • New batches are pending: the company may submit or delete.
  • After submit, batches are final for banks; the corporate cannot delete them anymore.
  • Banks only see submitted batches. Pending stays internal.

Employee payments

  • Dedicated menu, separate from upload.
  • For corporates: Pending vs Submitted batches; open payment lines.
  • For banks: submitted batches for their corporates, with payout lines.

Not in scope for this demo

  • Formal approvals, reviewer vs operator, activation email, MFA, production security posture.

Honest limits

  • No uptime or SLA commitments.
  • Walkthrough / concept validation—not a finished bank-grade product.
